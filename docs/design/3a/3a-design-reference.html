DESIGN SPEC · 3A · WRITTEN SO A NEW PAGE CAN BE BUILT FROM IT ALONE
1 · CORE CONCEPT

Financbro is a German business-school quiz app dressed as a private bank. The premise carries every decision: knowledge is money, a question is a posting on your account, answering correctly is a credit, your skill level is a tier, and your future is a dead-end career you pick from a menu. The comedy comes from banking vocabulary applied with total sincerity to a student cramming annuities at 01:40 — never from cartoon graphics. Rule of thumb: the layout should be able to pass as a real bank; only the words are allowed to be funny.

2 · NAVIGATION

One navy bar, 64 px tall, on every page. Brand sits left (wordmark + bobbing 💵 + a mono “PRIVATE CLIENT · SEIT 2026” kicker). Everything else is pushed right by a flex spacer: five section links, a hairline divider, then the balance pill. Links are emoji-suffixed and title-cased (Quiz 🧠, Bro Shop 💸, Multiplayer 🥋, Language 🎤, Career 🪦); the active one is a solid green chip, the rest are pale blue text on navy. The balance pill (💵 1,340 · TIER 4 📚) is the only element that ever animates in the chrome: a +180 floats up out of it on a credit. On phone the same bar collapses to brand + pill + ☰, and the five links become a fixed bottom tab bar with emoji-over-label icons.

3 · THREE-COLUMN GRID

Desktop is 1440 wide: 18 px gaps, 22 px page padding. Left 200 px — ads only, and only ads, so a slow or ugly creative can never touch the reading column. Centre, fluid ~860 px — the work: progress strip, question card, one leaderboard slot. Right 300 px — the account: balance, activity ledger, career track. Below 1200 px the ad rail drops out entirely (ads reappear as one in-feed card), below 900 px the account rail folds into the header. Nothing in the centre column ever reflows because of an ad.

4 · COLOUR, TYPE, MOTION

Field #eef1f5, cards #fff, hairlines #dde4ec, body text #0f2137, secondary #5d7793. Navy #0f2137 is chrome; #16304b is the raised chip inside navy. One banking green #1c6b45 means correct + primary action; mint #7fd6a3 is its only-on-navy sibling; burnt orange #c2410c / #ff7a3d means wrong or streak, and appears at most twice per screen. No other hues, no gradients except the scroll fade under the phone button. Manrope 400/700/800 with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on every number; IBM Plex Mono only for formulas, labels in caps, and ad slot copy — so a formula can never be mistaken for prose. Radii: 999 px pills, 14 px cards, 10 px inputs. Motion is bank-like: balance counts up over ~500 ms, +180 floats 14 px and fades, the current progress segment breathes, the logo 💵 bobs. Nothing bounces, nothing confettis.

5 · PROGRESS SYSTEM (PHONE IS THE FULL VERSION)

Progress is a stack of four facts inside one raised navy card, not a single bar: (a) course name plus a tally — 6 ✓ / 1 ✗ / 3 left; (b) one segment per question, green answered-right, orange answered-wrong, navy untouched, and the current segment taller, outlined mint and slowly pulsing; (c) streak line — 🔥 4 streak · ×1.5 multiplier next to a mono “POSTING 08 OF 10”; (d) below a hairline, the long-game tier bar with “660 💵 to 🧳”. Desktop compresses (a)–(c) into one white strip above the question card and moves (d) into the account rail. The lesson: session progress and career progress are two different clocks and are always drawn separately.

6 · MONEY, CAREERS, MODES

The currency is written as a number followed by 💵 — never “BD”, never a word. Numeric questions pay 180, multiple choice 120, a boss fight 900; a wrong answer pays 0 and is logged as a write-off. Careers are paid perks with a joke and a real mechanic each (Crypto Guy: ×3 or ×0 at random; Big 4 Auditor: tolerance ±0.01 %; Startup Founder: costs 1 💵, pays out “at exit”), so the humour is also the difficulty setting. Session setup is one dropdown of named modes (Klausur-Panik · 10, Quickie · 5, Semester-Marathon · ∞, Speedrun · 60 s, Boss fight) plus a tickbox list of courses with question counts, a live “131 questions selected · est. 24 min” line, and one green Start earning 💵 button. Course selection is multi-select ticks, mode is single-select — never mixed into one control.

7 · ADS · THE DEAL

The app is free and ad-funded and says so out loud (“Monthly account fee — 0 · we sell ads”). Every slot is a card with a hairline border, a caps SPONSORED/AD label, and a fixed height, drawn as diagonal-striped placeholders with mono copy stating the exact pixel size and why the slot exists. Sizes: 160×600 + 160×160 in the left rail, 970×60 under the question card, 300×100 in the phone feed, and one 468×76 sponsored-career card in the career grid — which is the sharpest joke in the product, because a real employer's card looks identical to “Excel Monkey”. Ads never sit above the answer field, never overlap chrome, never move layout.

8 · VOICE · AND WHAT IT COSTS

Write like a bank statement with a hangover: short, declarative, German-English code-switching where it lands (Klausur-Panik, Sparkassen-Beamter, Sprache), one joke per surface and never two in a row. Explanations stay strictly correct — the maths is the one thing that is never a bit. Punchlines live in microcopy: “one write-off, we do not talk about posting 03”, “until you cry”, “dies with your cache”. Worse at: because the shell is deliberately a credible bank, the parody is quiet — a screenshot without copy reads as fintech, and the ad rail costs real screen width on 1280 laptops.
